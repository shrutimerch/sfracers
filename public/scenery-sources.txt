South Park scenery references

Mapped park boundary, buildings, tree positions and pedestrian paths:
OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, retrieved September 2026.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Park paving, landscape and furniture design reference:
https://www.fletcher.studio/southpark
https://www.sfrecpark.org/576/South-Park

164 South Park facade reference:
https://www.saitowitz.com/164-south-park

The user's street-level South Park screenshot guided colors, trees, planted edges,
concrete slabs, gravel and furniture proportions. No Google photographs are
embedded, copied into textures or served by this game.

Building footprints and available mapped heights are real map geometry.
Unmapped heights, window patterns, facade colors, furnishings, vegetation shape
and parked cars are approximations. This is a reference-based 3D reconstruction,
not photogrammetry or an address-by-address photographic survey.
